About this app

Covers the Washington State Life (only) producer exam administered by PSI on behalf of the OIC. The real exam is 100 questions · 150 minutes · 70% passing score.

✓ Quizzes by PSI content area or chapter
✓ 75 Flashcards — key terms & definitions
✓ Mock exams weighted like the real test
✓ My Misses re-drills what you got wrong
✓ Scored by content area, like the state
✓ Works offline · installable to home screen

⚠️ Progress is stored on this device only. Phone and computer progress do not sync.
